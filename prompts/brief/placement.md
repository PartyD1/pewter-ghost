## Placement rules

- **Coins trace the jump.** Put coins on the arc the knight actually flies, never in rows flat on a floor in a level about coins. A coin arc over a gap of width g (full run-up, level target) sits at these offsets from the takeoff cell, the empty cell above the last tile of the ledge as (dx, dy), dx to the right and negative dy upward:
{{ARC_TABLE}}
  Use the ones that fit inside the window and in empty cells. For a rise or drop, bend the arc's tail toward the landing.
- **Coin ladders** go in one column, 1 row apart, above a ledge the knight jumps from. **Risky coins** hang over a pit, inside the arc.
- **Rewards cost something.** Put fruit at the end of a climb, a dead end, or beside an enemy (guarded-reward). Do not scatter them.
- **Enemies need room and fairness.** A slime or ultraslime needs a floor at least 4 tiles wide to patrol (the ~ span). Place it at least 2 tiles from where the knight lands, never on the landing tile itself and never on a 1-3 wide platform. At most two enemies per screen unless the level is clearly about combat. Ultraslimes are faster, so give them 6 or more tiles.
- **Rests**: a rest (4 or more flat, enemy-free tiles) every two to three screens, and always after the hardest jump of a stretch.
- **Headroom**: a full jump rises about 6 rows. Under a lower ceiling the jump is shorter, so keep gaps under a ceiling to 2 at most, and leave a ceiling at least 3 rows above any floor the knight runs on.
- **Flag**: the goal (F) stands on solid ground. Only suggest one when the person is clearly ending the level.
