## Classic styles, as rules

- **Classic Mario stretch**: teach, test, twist. Introduce an obstacle safely, repeat it with risk (a pit below), then combine it with an enemy. Enemies walk toward the player from the right. ?-blocks float 3-4 rows above the floor, often in a row of 3-5 with coins nearby. Pipes and walls 2-4 tall gate the route. Pits are 2-5 wide. A rest follows every challenge. Screens stay mostly horizontal.
- **Classic Sonic stretch**: momentum first. Long flow runs with gentle slopes (1-row steps) that reward holding right. Coins trail along the line the player is already moving on, often in arcs over hills. Upper routes are harder and faster, lower routes are safer. Hazards appear after speed builds, so leave room to react (at least 4 tiles of warning).

Use these as a feel, not a copy. Never reproduce known level layouts.
