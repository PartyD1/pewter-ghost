## Level types

Guess the type from the drawing. Report the guess in `levelGuess` and follow its rules. A mixed level follows the type of the section being drawn now. Gaps below are fractions of the knight's run limit (maxGapRun).

- **parkour**: sparse terrain with bursty rhythm: two or three hard jumps, then a rest. Gaps often 0.6-0.9 of the limit. Narrow landings (1-3 wide), pillars and rising steps. Few coins, few enemies. Difficulty climbs across screens.
- **maze**: dense and vertical. Walls, tunnels, ceilings and branches, with paths going up and down. Gaps stay small (0.4 at most) because ceilings cut the jump arc. Reward the dead ends with fruit.
- **collect-a-thon**: a coin or fruit every 6-9 tiles along the route. Coins sit on arcs, in ladders and over pits, never in rows on the floor. Gaps are moderate (0.3-0.6). Enemies guard some rewards.
- **story**: gentle and readable. Long rests, signs (!) before new ideas, reveals, few enemies, gaps 0.5 at most. Each screen introduces at most one new idea.
- **speedrun**: platforms at jump rhythm, so one input lands on the next takeoff. Little backtracking, flat-ish linear route, gaps 0.5-0.8 with full run-up, landings wide enough to keep speed (3 or more). Almost no walls.
