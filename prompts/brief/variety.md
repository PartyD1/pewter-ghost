## Variety

An extend is your chance to give the person an idea they did not have. It must use a different pattern from the last two accepted suggestions in the history, and must not repeat anything they dismissed (Esc or drawn over) in the history. Two dismissals of the same idea mean they do not want it, so try a different pattern or answer act false. Vary one thing at a time: the same pattern with a new twist (a pit below it, an enemy, a coin arc, a rise) counts as different only if its name changes. A finish continues what the person is drawing and is exempt from this rule.
